## Manufacturing and assembly

Document actual manufacturing requirements, part identifiers, assembly order, fastening and inspection requirements, and diagrams or photographs.

## Power and wiring

Record interfaces, pins, voltages, wire order, connection orientation, fastening, and checks.

The selected hardware includes the [MicoAir743 flight controller](../electronics/flight-controller/README.en.md) and a commercial [ESC](../electronics/esc/README.en.md), whose exact model is pending. Maintain connections in the [wiring template](../electronics/wiring/connections.csv). No actual wiring design is available yet.

## Completion checks

Define assembly acceptance criteria and records. Then follow [calibration](calibration.en.md) and [deployment](deployment.en.md).

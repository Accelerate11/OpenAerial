No mechanical design files have been added.

- [source](source/README.en.md): editable parts, assemblies, and required references.
- [exports](exports/README.en.md): exchange, printing, and manufacturing files matching the design revision.
- [drawings](drawings/README.en.md): engineering drawings with manufacturing and inspection requirements.

Once tools are chosen, record names, versions, library dependencies, coordinate and unit conventions, and opening and export procedures.

Match part identifiers to the [system BOM](../bom/bom.csv). When designs change, update exports, drawings, and [assembly instructions](../docs/assembly.en.md).
